Help

Make a card in four steps

  1. Click the mic and say the question.
  2. Say “flip card” or click Flip Card (or press F).
  3. Say the answer.
  4. Say “save card” or click Save Card (S). A blank card is ready for the next one.

Voice commands

flip cardSwitch sides (also “switch sides”, or just “flip” on its own) go to backDictate into the back · go to front for the front save cardSave and start the next card (also “next card”) scratch thatRemove the last phrase clear cardEmpty both sides (you can undo) blank … end blankHide those words (makes a cloze card) add tag …Add a one-word tag, e.g. “add tag PPH” comma · full stop · new linePunctuation (Settings)

Commands are taken out of the text, never typed onto the card. Pause briefly before and after a command for the most reliable results.

Keyboard

F flip · S save · M start/pause dictation (when you are not typing in a box)

Getting cards into Anki

  • Any device: click Download file for Anki, then in Anki desktop choose File › Import. Deck, note type and tags come from the file. AnkiWeb sync carries the cards to your phone. Then click Mark as sent.
  • Anki open on this computer: install the free AnkiConnect add-on (code 2055492159), keep Anki open, click Add to Anki on this computer, and approve the permission dialog in Anki the first time.
  • iPhone (AnkiMobile): card menu (⋮) › AnkiMobile.
  • Android (AnkiDroid): card menu (⋮) › Share › AnkiDroid.

Tick cards in the list to send only those. A card is marked “Sent” only after Anki confirms it, or after you click Mark as sent.

Privacy

Cards are kept only in this browser. Dictation uses your browser’s speech service (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari). Don’t dictate patient information. Firefox has no built-in dictation; use your keyboard’s mic key there.

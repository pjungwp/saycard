Send to Anki

Any device: download the file, then in Anki desktop choose File › Import. Cards sync to your phone through AnkiWeb.

Anki desktop open on this computer: “Add to Anki” sends cards directly (needs the free AnkiConnect add-on, code 2055492159).

iPhone with AnkiMobile: tap “AnkiMobile” on a card to add it there.

Android: tap “Share” on a card and choose AnkiDroid.

Your cards

Cards are saved only on this device until you send them to Anki. Voice is turned into text by your browser’s speech service (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari), so don’t dictate patient information.
